Capabilities

From raw log sheets to explainable early warning

◎

Anomaly detection

Isolation Forest, dense autoencoder and a temporal-window autoencoder score every hourly record against each substation's own learned behaviour.

⚡

Operational risk scoring

Model evidence fused with engineering rules — voltage collapse, incomer drop-outs, thermal residuals — into a 0–100 risk indicator with confidence.

⌁

Equipment intelligence

A digital substation built from the log-sheet structure: buses, transformers, feeders and couplers, each coloured by its contributing signals.

◈

Investigation workspace

What happened, why it was flagged, how unusual it is and what an engineer should check — backed by the source measurements.

▦

Fleet analytics

Voltage, loading and temperature health across substations, anomaly timelines, equipment loading against nameplate rating.

⟲

Historical replay

Real records replayed through the live inference path at 1×–10×. Live SCADA ingestion is not connected — and the product says so.

Research

Honest validation on real substation data

Out-of-sample by design

Every day of HVPNL data is scored only by models fitted on the other days — reference statistics, feature selection, scaling and calibration included. No leakage, no fabricated labels, no accuracy claims where no labels exist.

Real events, real evidence

On 02 Feb 2026 10:00 the 11 kV T-I voltage at 220 kV Sector-46 was logged at 0 kV; on 10 Feb 2026 08:00 the T-2 incomer and several feeders dropped to 0 A while T-4 loading rose. Both surface as high-risk investigations from unseen-day models.

Stated limits

Three non-consecutive days, hourly manual logs, six of twenty-four sheets with readings. Risk categories are a prototype AI classification — not protection settings.

Security

Built like an operational system

  • JWT authentication with ADMIN · ENGINEER · OPERATOR · VIEWER roles
  • PBKDF2 password hashing — no plaintext credentials
  • Rate limiting, input validation, secure headers, CORS allow-list
  • Audit log for logins, replay control, exports and investigation notes
  • Secrets only via environment variables
  • Private datasets never committed to source control
